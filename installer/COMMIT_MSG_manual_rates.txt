Manual sample rate and RF bandwidth, bandwidth defaults to None, Pluto gain limit

- Sample rate and RF bandwidth each get a "Manual" entry that takes any value
  (MS/s and MHz). The preset entries stay.
- RF bandwidth starts on None on every launch; a satellite choice no longer
  moves it. None sends no bandwidth limit: the Pluto opens its analog filter to
  follow the sample rate, RTL-SDR and HackRF use their automatic bandwidth.
- A manual PlutoSDR bandwidth outside 0.2-56 MHz is clamped instead of refused.
- IQ playback: Max plays the full file, and Range defaults to Full file.
- PlutoSDR: a gain above what the AD9361 accepts at the tuned frequency (about
  71 dB near 1.7 GHz) no longer stops the receiver from starting. It is stepped
  down to the highest accepted value and the status bar says so.
